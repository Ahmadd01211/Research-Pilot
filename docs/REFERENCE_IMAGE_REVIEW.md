## Immediate changes in this slice

The real auth/profile pages adopt the approved calm typography, white/gray surfaces, navy hierarchy, self-hosted fonts, restrained blue controls and clear save/session feedback. The authenticated dashboard is honest about its current scope; it does not copy the examples' feeds or counts.

## Later feature guidance

When implementing library, matrix and writing slices, refer to the relevant images above alongside `design-export/DESIGN.md`. Keep the approved primary navigation and evidence-first rules. Use the images as interaction references, not exact replicas: branding, example paper content, statistics and unrelated product claims are not reused.

Tradeoff: side panels reduce navigation and keep evidence near decisions, but consume reading width. Desktop can use contextual panels; tablet/mobile should stack or open a drawer. Dense real tables support comparison, but need horizontal scrolling, sticky study labels and keyboard-accessible evidence actions.
